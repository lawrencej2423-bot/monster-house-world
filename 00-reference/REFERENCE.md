# REFERENCE — The Real Monster House (2006 film) — SOURCE OF TRUTH FOR THE FACADE

**Status: This file supersedes every earlier facade description** (DESIGN.md §2's generic
"angry house," the Space illustration, and any facade manifest written before it). The user
rejected the generic version and set the target: a **~95% replica of the actual house from
the 2006 animated film *Monster House***. Facade, layout, and assets crews: build against
the checklist in §3 of this file, item by item, and measure with the ratios given — do not
freestyle proportions.

All ratios below use **W = width of the house body** (siding wall to siding wall, porch
excluded) and **H = total height, ground to roof ridge**. Measured off the poster and film
stills listed in §1; treat ratios as ±10% tolerances, not millimetre specs.

## 2. Feature breakdown of the real house

### Overall form & proportions
- A **two-storey** wooden house, but the **roof mass dominates**: the steep shingled roof
  occupies roughly the **top 40% of H**. Wall storeys read as: porch band ≈ bottom 25% of
  H, upper wall band (with the eyes) ≈ 20% of H, roof ≈ 40% of H, with the sagging
  shingle skirt/brow band making up the transition (~15%).
- **H ≈ 1.0–1.1 × W.** The house is nearly as tall as it is wide — much taller and
  narrower than a normal suburban house. It leans/sags subtly; nothing is level.
- The whole front is **symmetrical in structure, asymmetrical in detail**: two eyes, but
  the left eye is set in the flat wall and the right eye in a projecting dormer; the
  siding warps toward the centre.

### Roof & chimney
- **Steep, tall shingled roof**, roughly pyramidal/hipped from the front with a short
  ridge; shingles are large, uneven, hand-laid-looking wooden shakes in dark
  blue-grey, many curled, lifted, or missing — in monster mode the roof surface tears
  into jagged holes.
- Eaves **sag in the middle**, dipping toward the centre of the facade — this sag plus
  the shingle skirt below it forms the **furrowed brow** over the eyes.
- **Chimney: small, dark brick, just left of centre near the ridge** (clearly visible
  with a smoke wisp in the poster #1/#2). It is a minor accent, not a big stack.

### Siding & weathering
- **Horizontal wooden plank siding**, desaturated **blue-grey / weathered grey**
  (reads almost slate-blue at night), heavily weathered: warped, bowed, splintered,
  gaps between boards, no two boards aligned.
- Between and below the eye windows the planks **twist and converge toward a centre
  point** (the "nose bridge" above the porch-roof peak) — long wrinkle lines radiate
  from that point. This convergence is a defining feature; straight flat siding reads
  wrong immediately.

### The eyes (upper windows)
- **Exactly TWO lit windows on the upper front. No other front windows glow.**
- **Count/placement:** one left, one right, in the upper wall band just under the brow.
  Centres at approximately **0.35 W and 0.65 W** — **eye spacing ≈ 0.30–0.35 W**.
  Each window ≈ **0.09–0.11 W wide** and slightly taller than wide.
- **Left eye:** set flat in the main wall. Poster art gives it a **slightly arched
  top**; film stills (#6) show it squarish with a **cross muntin** (one vertical, one
  horizontal bar). Build: square window, cross muntins, with a shallow arch trim on top
  to satisfy both.
- **Right eye:** a **square 2×2 four-pane window** set in a **small projecting dormer
  box** that juts forward out of the roof slope on the right side. The dormer is a
  defining asymmetry — do not mirror the left window.
- **Glow colour: warm orange/amber** (approx. #FF9A2A–#FFB84D), bright at the centre of
  each pane, with a soft halo spilling onto the surrounding siding. Both eyes glow at
  rest AND in monster mode — the glow is the "alive" signal.

### Brow
- Formed by three stacked cues, top to bottom: (1) the sagging roof eave dipping toward
  centre, (2) a band of **ragged shingle skirt** hanging below the eave like a fringe,
  (3) the twisted converging siding wrinkles under the eyes. Together they read as a
  permanent scowl. In the poster the mid-facade shingle band is visibly **broken and
  splintered at its centre**, directly above the nose point.

### Front door & porch (at rest)
- **Full-width porch** across the entire front (≈ 1.0–1.05 W, ends flush or a hair past
  the body), shallow depth ≈ 0.10–0.12 W, with a **low-pitched shingled porch roof**
  that has a **slight centre peak** (the peak sits under the nose point).
- Porch structure: **thin vertical posts/screen frames** in a regular rhythm
  (≈ one every 0.10–0.12 W), a **low simple railing**, dark screened openings between
  posts. The porch interior is in deep shadow.
- **Front door: centred (≈ 0.47–0.50 W)**, set in the wall behind the porch, shown
  **open/ajar with intense warm yellow-white light** (approx. #FFD76A core) pouring
  out in a bright rectangle and spilling a light pool onto the porch floor and down
  the walkway. This door-light is the brightest element in the whole scene.

### The monster mouth (transformation)
- The **porch becomes the mouth** — not the door, not a new opening. The porch roof
  lifts/tilts up like an upper lip; the porch floor drops slightly as the lower jaw.
- **Mouth spans ≈ 0.85 W** (nearly the full porch width) and ≈ the full porch height.
- **Teeth are the house's own splintered wood**: long, irregular, jagged boards and
  shingle shards hang from the porch-roof edge as **upper teeth** (longest ≈ 35–40% of
  mouth height, lengths deliberately uneven, several broken short), and shorter jagged
  boards splinter **up from the porch floor as lower teeth** (≈ 25–30% of mouth
  height). No clean, even, or white teeth — all teeth are grey-blue splintered timber,
  angled slightly inward.
- Mouth interior is **near-black**; in several stills a dark red-brown depth is barely
  visible. The door and its light are gone/inside the mouth in this mode.
- Optional film-accurate extras (climax only, NOT the default yard backdrop): tree-limb
  arms on the sides, house lifted on root/branch legs, dust at the base. Default for
  our world: **house planted, mouth open, no arms** unless the design lead says
  otherwise.

### Yard, walkway, trees, signs
- **Walkway: one straight concrete path on the centre line** (≈ 0.5 W), width
  ≈ 0.07–0.09 W, running from the street/curb straight to the porch steps. No curves,
  no branches. In the poster it catches the door light.
- **Trees: at least TWO massive bare gnarled trees frame the house**, trunks at
  roughly ±0.6–0.7 W from centre, trunk width ≈ 0.07–0.09 W, with twisted leafless
  branches reaching over/around the house. Behind and between them, **more bare trees**
  pack both sides densely (the house sits in a dead grove, not an open lawn). One
  smaller tree with sparse autumn-brown leaves appears at the left in some stills.
- **Lawn signs (film still #4):** two small hand-painted boards on short stakes at the
  front lawn edge — **"KEEP AWAY" on the left** (near the left trees, ≈ 0.25 W) and
  **"BEWARE" on the right of the walkway** (≈ 0.60 W). Rough dark boards, crude
  light lettering, slightly crooked. Sign height ≈ 0.04 H.
- **Lawn:** in the poster, a muted dark-green lawn strip between walkway and street;
  in film stills the yard reads deader — patchy brown/olive. Use dark desaturated
  green-brown, mostly in shadow.

### Night palette (use these, not generic "spooky" colours)
- Sky/ambient: deep blue-teal night (#0B1D2A – #14313F), moonlit pale-blue rim light
  from behind/above (full moon behind the roof in the poster).
- Shingles: darkest blue-grey (#2E3B44 range). Siding: mid blue-grey (#54646E range),
  weathering darker at board ends.
- Eye glow: orange-amber #FF9A2A–#FFB84D. Door light: yellow-white #FFD76A core.
- Trees: near-black blue-grey silhouettes with pale rim light.
- Ground mist/dust: pale blue-grey, low at the base (monster-mode stills).

## 4. Where the references disagree (flagged, with the ruling)

1. **Chimney:** prominent with smoke in the poster (#1/#2); not visible in the street
   still (#4) — the roof top is cropped/dark there, not proof of absence. **Ruling:
   include it, small, left of ridge.**
2. **Left eye shape:** arched top in poster art; squarish with cross muntins in film
   still #6. **Ruling: square window + cross muntins + shallow arch trim (both reads
   satisfied).**
3. **Lawn colour:** dark green in the poster; dead brown/olive in film stills.
   **Ruling: desaturated green-brown mix, shadow-dominant.**
4. **Porch vs mouth width:** at rest the porch reads slightly narrower than the body
   in the poster; in monster mode the mouth clearly spans nearly the full body width.
   **Ruling: porch ≈ 1.0 W; mouth ≈ 0.85 W inside it.**
5. **Right eye height:** in low-angle monster stills (#3) the dormer eye reads higher
   than the left eye — that is perspective from the low camera plus the dormer's
   projection, not a higher placement. **Ruling: eyes on the same level; the dormer
   projects forward, not up.**
